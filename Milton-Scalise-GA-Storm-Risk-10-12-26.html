03

Scoring system

Public federal data at census-tract resolution

What goes into the score
  • TornadoNRI tornado risk, 30% of the score.
  • HailNRI hail risk, 25%.
  • Strong windNRI strong wind risk (thunderstorm and straight-line wind), 25%.
  • HurricaneNRI hurricane risk, 20%. Coastal flooding is flagged separately in section 07.
  • TiersSet within Georgia by housing: the top 10% of homes are Extreme, the next 20% High, the next 30% Moderate and the rest Low.

04

Home campaign: recommended treatment

Keep every tier in the campaign; change the conversation in High and Extreme

What to do with the tiers
  • No exclusionsWind and hail are insurable everywhere in Georgia; we do not recommend removing any ZIP from the Home campaign on this score.
  • DeductiblesIn High and Extreme ZIPs, explain the wind and hail deductible at quote time, before the first spring storm season.
  • Roof questionsAsk roof age and material on every High and Extreme quote; it is the first thing underwriting checks after a storm.
  • AppetiteIf Allstate restricts new home business in any county, tell us and we will remove it in GOAL.

  • Risk sourceFEMA National Risk Index, census tract table, December 2025 release (tornado, hail, strong wind, hurricane, coastal and inland flooding, community resilience), 2,791 Georgia tracts.
